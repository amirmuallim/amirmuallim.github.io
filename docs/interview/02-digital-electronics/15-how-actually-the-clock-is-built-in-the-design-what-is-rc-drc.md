# How actually the clock is built in the design, what is RC, DRC, erc, lvs?

## Answer

The clock is generated by a clock source such as a PLL or oscillator, but in physical design the important step is Clock Tree Synthesis. CTS takes the clock source and builds a buffered clock distribution network to drive the clock pins of all sequential elements. Buffers and inverters are inserted and sized to control clock skew, insertion delay, transition and power.

RC refers to the resistance and capacitance of the interconnect. Since real wires have parasitic R and C, they introduce delay and signal-integrity effects. After routing, parasitic extraction determines these values so that accurate timing analysis can be performed.

DRC stands for Design Rule Check and verifies that the physical layout follows the foundry's manufacturing rules such as minimum width and spacing. LVS stands for Layout Versus Schematic and checks whether the connectivity extracted from the layout matches the intended netlist. ERC stands for Electrical Rule Check and checks electrical-rule violations such as floating or improperly connected nodes. So, simply, DRC checks manufacturability, LVS checks whether we built the intended circuit, and ERC checks electrical correctness.
