# Define clock skew.

## Answer

Clock skew is the difference in the arrival time of the same clock edge at two different sequential elements (flip-flops) in a circuit.

> **Two types**

- **Positive skew:** Capture FF receives the clock **later** than the launch FF.

- **Negative skew:** Capture FF receives the clock **earlier** than the launch FF.
