# PVT

## Answer

PVT stands for Process, Voltage and Temperature. These are the three major sources of variation that affect semiconductor circuit behavior. Process represents manufacturing variations, commonly represented by corners such as SS, FF and TT. Voltage represents variations in the supply voltage, and temperature represents the operating temperature range. We analyze the design at relevant PVT corners because cell delay, power and timing can change significantly with these conditions. In physical design and STA, checking multiple PVT corners helps ensure that setup, hold and other specifications are met across the required operating conditions
