# Which of the following diodes exhibits negative resistance due to quantum tunneling?


Tunnel diode

🧠 Explanation (Concise):

A Tunnel Diode shows negative resistance due to the quantum tunneling effect. At very small junction widths, electrons "tunnel" through the potential barrier instead of overcoming it, resulting in a region where current decreases as voltage increases — i.e., negative differential resistance.
